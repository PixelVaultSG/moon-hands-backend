# MOON HANDS — 45-SECOND HERO VIDEO
## Production Brief & Storyboard
### For: Aesthetics Clinic Decision-Makers (Owners, Clinic Managers)
### Format: Screen Recording + Motion Graphics | No Actors | Neutral Demo
### Duration: 45 seconds | Target: Website hero, LinkedIn, sales deck opener

---

## 1. STRATEGIC POSITIONING

### The One-Sentence Pitch
"Moon Hands is the only AI receptionist built specifically for aesthetics clinics — it books patients into your calendar while your team is treating other patients, eating lunch, or sleeping."

### The Problem We Open With
Aesthetics clinics in Singapore lose 30-40% of potential bookings because:
- Patients message at 11 PM, during lunch, or while your front desk is on break
- Front desk staff are too busy checking in patients to reply to WhatsApp fast enough
- No-shows cost $200-500 per appointment because patients forget

### The Audience Mindset
This person is a clinic owner or manager. They are:
- Already spending $3K-8K/month on ads to get enquiries
- Losing sleep over missed WhatsApp messages
- Skeptical of "AI chatbots" (they've seen clunky ones)
- Wants to see the actual product, not a cartoon

### The Emotional Arc
1. **Tension** (0-5s) — "Your patients are messaging right now. Are you replying?"
2. **Relief** (5-20s) — Watch the AI handle a full booking in 30 seconds
3. **Delight** (20-35s) — Staff gets instant Telegram alert. Calendar auto-fills.
4. **Confidence** (35-45s) — "Moon Hands. Never miss a booking."

### PILLAR 1: NEVER MISS A BOOKING

**Visual proof:** Real WhatsApp screen recording showing sub-second reply speed.

### PILLAR 2: ZERO STAFF DISRUPTION
**The promise:** Your team never leaves their workflow. Everything flows to a clean Telegram dashboard. One tap to pause, approve, or take over.

**The promise:** Patient gets an instant calendar invite. No-shows drop because the appointment is in their Apple/Google/Outlook calendar with clinic details, address, and prep instructions.
**Visual proof:** Calendar event popping into iPhone Calendar with all details pre-filled.

## 5. MOTION DESIGN DIRECTION

### Kinetics & Timing (Reference: Linear + Stripe)
- **Entry transitions:** 0.15-0.25s. Snappy. No slow fades.
- **Exit transitions:** 0.35s. Slightly slower than entry — feels deliberate.
- **Text animations:** Kinetic type (letters fly in individually) for headlines. Typewriter for body.
- **UI card animations:** Slide from bottom with 0.08s stagger between items.
- **Zoom effects:** 1.05x scale on interactive elements (buttons, cards) on hover/appear.
- **Split-screen wipe:** 30° diagonal, 0.4s duration, ease-in-out.

### Typography System
- **Headlines:** Inter Bold or SF Pro Display Bold, 64-96pt
- **Body / UI text:** Inter Regular or SF Pro Text, 14-18pt
- **Data / prices:** SF Mono or JetBrains Mono, 16pt (monospace feels clinical and precise)
- **Accent text:** Playfair Display Italic or Cormorant Garamond (for luxury moments)

### Visual Effects
- **Gold shimmer:** On CTAs and price highlights. Subtle CSS-like shimmer, not Vegas.
- **Glow pulses:** Behind AI reply bubbles. Soft radial gradient, 0.5s pulse cycle.
- **Particle dust:** Tiny floating gold dots (2-3px) drifting across staff dashboard scenes. Adds premium texture.
- **Depth of field:** Slight blur on background elements during focus moments (WhatsApp chat sharp, rest soft).
