6 Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a consultation before treatment?
A complimentary consultation is required for all injectable treatments (Botox, fillers, Rejuran, thread lift). Facials, peels, and laser treatments can be booked directly without consultation.
Is there downtime?
Most facials and peels have zero downtime. Injectables may have mild redness or swelling for 24-48 hours. HIFU and thread lifts may have slight swelling for 2-3 days. Your doctor will advise during consultation.
How do I prepare for my appointment?
Avoid alcohol 24 hours before injectables. Come with clean skin (no makeup). Inform us of any medications or allergies. Arrive 10 minutes early to complete registration.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept PayNow, credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), and cash. Installment plans available for treatments above $1,000 via Atome or Grab PayLater.
Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?
Yes! You can reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours before your appointment at no charge. Late cancellations (within 24 hours) may incur a $50 fee.
Are your doctors certified?
Yes, all our doctors are MOH-certified aesthetic physicians with minimum 5 years of experience. Dr. Amanda Chen (Medical Director) has 15+ years in aesthetic medicine.
